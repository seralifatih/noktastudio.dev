Pixel Raid

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Pixel Raid, a side-scrolling shooter published by Nokta Studio under the package name dev.noktastudio.pixelraid. The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not designed for children.

Effective date: October 26, 2026 Developer: Nokta Studio Contact: h.f.ilhan@gmail.com

What stays on your device

Your top-5 scores, your initials, your sound setting, your saved run, the highest stage you reached, and whether you own "Remove Ads" are stored only on your device. We run no server, the game has no accounts, and we never receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Nokta Studio does not collect, receive, or sell any personal data from Pixel Raid. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising and store services below handle themselves.

Ads

Pixel Raid shows full-screen ads (interstitials) between runs, served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to protect against fraud, AdMob (Google) may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • usage data, such as ad interactions and how the app is used;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance information.

We do not receive this data and cannot identify you from it. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, and other regions where required): on first launch you are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. No ad is requested until you have made that choice. You can change it at any time from the NO ADS screen (PRIVACY button, shown where consent applies).
  • iOS tracking: after the consent step, iOS asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized.
  • Device settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising).
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase turns ads off permanently. Once ads are off, the game no longer requests ads or shows the consent form.

Purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment details are processed by Google or Apple; we never see or store them. The game only learns whether the purchase succeeded and keeps that fact on your device. You can restore the purchase on a new device with the same store account using the RESTORE button.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data and do not share any data ourselves. The only third parties involved are Google (ads) and Google or Apple (purchases), under their own privacy policies and retention rules. Data stored by the game on your device stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Children's privacy

Pixel Raid is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version at this URL and revise the effective date above.
