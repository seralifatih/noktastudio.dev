Featured insight

3 APIs for Turkish E-Commerce Intelligence on Apify

Turkish marketplace data is valuable, but hard to operationalize. Product pages, seller profiles, and review systems all expose different structures, which usually leaves teams stitching together brittle scrapers and cleanup scripts. I packaged that work into three production-ready Apify Actors that behave like APIs: send JSON input, get normalized output, and plug the results into BI pipelines, alerts, or AI workflows without a custom parser per marketplace.

N11 Product Scraper for structured catalog, pricing, seller, and stock data Seller Intelligence across Trendyol, Hepsiburada, and N11 Review Aggregator with normalized schema and Turkish sentiment tagging Built for pricing analysis, seller evaluation, competitor tracking, and research

Why I Prefer Small Products Over Big Platforms

I bias toward software that solves one job clearly, ships quickly, and earns its place in a real workflow. That constraint shapes everything from scope to pricing to the kinds of products Nokta Studio chooses to launch.

Reusing the same core stack across products is less about fashion and more about speed. Next.js, Cloudflare, Supabase, and AI-assisted workflows reduce context switching so new ideas get validated and deployed sooner.
